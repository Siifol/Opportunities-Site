About Opportunities.com

Opportunities.com helps job seekers and scholarship applicants find jobs, scholarships, and fellowships from around the world, starting with Ethiopia.

How it works

  1. Browse the listings on the home page, or search by title or organization.
  2. Create an account and add your skills, education, languages, and work experience.
  3. Upload your CV once. You can update it any time from your account.
  4. Apply to a listing. Your CV and details are shared with the team that posted it.

Our listings

Listings are posted by our team. Before you apply or pay any fee, always confirm the details on the official application link.

Languages

The site is available in English, Amharic, and Afaan Oromoo. Use the language button in the top bar to switch.
