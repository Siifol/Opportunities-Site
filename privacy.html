Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Opportunities.com helps job seekers and students in Ethiopia and around the world find jobs and scholarships. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and who can see it.

1. Information we collect

  • Your full name, username, email or phone number, and country.
  • Your skills, education, languages, and work experience.
  • Your CV file (PDF, DOC, or DOCX, up to 2 MB).
  • Your password, which is stored in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot read it.
  • The applications you send through this site, including any cover note you write.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To apply for opportunities on your behalf when you use the site application form.
  • To show you opportunities and keep track of the ones you applied for.

3. Who can see your information

  • Site administrators can see your profile, CV, and applications, so they can manage listings and check applications.
  • The hiring company receives your application, your profile details, and your CV by email, but only when you apply through the site application form for that opportunity.
  • Official links: if a listing sends you to an official website or asks you to apply by email or in person, we do not receive your application. Those companies handle your information under their own privacy rules.
  • We do not sell your information.

4. Login

When you log in, your browser keeps a login session for up to 30 days so you do not have to log in every time. You can log out at any time from your account page, which ends the session on that device.

5. Your choices

  • You can replace your CV at any time from the My CV page.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and information by contacting us. Use the email on our Contact page.

6. Keeping your information safe

We store your information on secure servers and protect passwords by hashing them. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong password and keep it private.
